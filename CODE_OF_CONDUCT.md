# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. We want FloatNote's issues and pull requests to be a friendly place for everyone.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you need.
- Keep to the topic; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Do not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" or contact a maintainer through the repository.
